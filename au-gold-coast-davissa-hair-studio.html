Our salon

Davissa Hair Studio is a family-owned and operated hair salon established on the Gold Coast in 2013. Owner Alicea started her journey as a hairdresser at 15 — today the team offers a warm, family-friendly salon experience, from a quick trim to a full transformation.

Services

Cut & Styling

Precision cuts, blow-drys and styling for women, men and kids — finished to perfection.

Colour & Correction

Full colour, regrowth and colour correction with premium non-toxic colour ranges.

Balayage

Hand-painted, sun-kissed balayage with beautiful blend and dimension.

Foils

Full and half-head foils for brightness, contrast and multi-tonal shine.

Treatments

Repairing and nourishing treatments that restore health and shine to your hair.

Special Occasions

Wedding and event styling — upstyles that photograph beautifully and last all day.
