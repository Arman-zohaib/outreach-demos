The little diner above the lumberyard.

Northern Cafe started in 1949 as a humble lunchroom serving hearty, affordable meals to dockworkers, truckers and tradespeople. Today, run by the Mah family, it's one of Vancouver's most beloved rooms — wallpapered with handwritten love letters from diners as far away as Hong Kong and Texas.

Come hungry. Leave happy. That's the whole philosophy.
