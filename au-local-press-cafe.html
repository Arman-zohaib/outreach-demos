About us

The Local Press serves healthy, hearty meals to eat in or take away. We're talking REAL FOOD — unrefined, unprocessed and free of unnecessary additives and preservatives. Our animal proteins are free-range, pasture-fed and chemical & hormone-free, and our ingredients are made in-house whenever possible using seasonal local produce.
