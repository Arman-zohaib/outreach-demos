About us

Willow House Coffee is a community coffee shop at 1004 South Saunders Street in Raleigh. Named for the willow tree — a symbol of strength, resilience and shelter — it is a gathering space for connection, with thoughtfully brewed coffee, wholesome food, and events that bring neighbors together.
