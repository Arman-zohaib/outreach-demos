About us

Straight Up Coffee + Food has served Hobart since 2014 with a 100% gluten-free and vegetarian menu. The busy kitchen team prepares every dish on site with a root-to-tip, low-waste approach — partnering with Baked Gluten Free for fresh bakes and Lottie Lane Coffee Roasters for the beans.
