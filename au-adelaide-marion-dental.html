About us

Marion Dental Clinic is a family dental practice conveniently located on Marion Road near Marion Shopping Centre, proudly serving Marion, Mitchell Park, Oaklands Park and surrounding southern Adelaide suburbs. Evening appointments Monday and Thursday until 7pm.

Services

Children Dentistry

Gentle dental care for kids — check-ups, cleans, fissure seals and early prevention.

Comprehensive Exams

Full dental examinations with x-rays to catch issues early and keep your smile healthy.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Tooth-coloured fillings, veneers and whitening for a confident, natural smile.

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-feeling replacements for missing teeth, from single tooth to full arches.

Crowns & Bridges

Strong, natural-looking restorations that rebuild damaged or missing teeth.

Root Canal Treatment

Comfortable, modern endodontic care to save teeth and relieve pain.
